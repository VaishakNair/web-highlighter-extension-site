Thank You for Installing Web Highlighter

Already have tabs open? Give them a quick refresh!

To start highlighting on tabs that were already open before you installed the extension, simply reload them with Ctrl + R (or Cmd + R on macOS).

📌 Pin to Your Toolbar

Keep Web Highlighter always 1-click away while browsing and researching:

1
Click the Extensions puzzle icon (🧩) in your Chrome toolbar.
2
Find Web Highlighter in your installed list.
3
Click the Pin icon (📌) so Web Highlighter stays permanently visible in your toolbar.
